**Author:** L. David Marquet

## Reflection

> "I intend to..."

Marquet took command of the USS Santa Fe, the worst-performing submarine in the fleet, and stopped giving orders. Instead of a leader-follower model, he pushed authority down to where the information already lived, and had his crew state intent rather than await instruction.

The mechanism is small and the effect is not. "I intend to submerge the ship" forces the person closest to the problem to think like the decision maker, while the captain still holds the veto. Competence and clarity have to come first, or handing over control is just abdication.
