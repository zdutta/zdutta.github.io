**Date Finished:** Mar 15, 2025

## Reflection

> "Lead with context, not control."

Hastings argues that freedom scales only after two things are in place: high talent density, and a culture of candour. Remove the weak performers, make feedback normal and direct, and only then can you start taking away the rules, the approval chains, and the expense policies.

Meyer's contribution is the useful counterweight. She shows where this breaks across cultures, and where the same candour that works in Amsterdam lands badly in Tokyo. The interesting part is not the no-vacation-policy headline, it is the sequencing: the controls come off last, not first.
